CodeBreaker 0.7.3 — Frontier legends

Add Emerald-only FRONTIER LEGENDS toggle, OFF by default. When enabled,
restricted legendary species pass both the Battle Frontier reception check
and the party picker. Normal species bans return immediately when disabled.
Other entry rules and Battle Factory rental selection remain unchanged.
Saved settings use the existing CodeBreaker toggle persistence.

Regression checks cover both entry paths, all seven facility IDs, both level
modes, turning the cheat off again, and unsupported-game fallthrough.
Previous Mega/alternate-form spawn fixes are retained.
